Gas safety principle

Gas hazard ≠ confined space. A gas hazardous area is classified because a flammable/toxic/asphyxiant gas atmosphere can exist; a confined space is classified by access, occupancy and physical characteristics. A location may be both, but the terms are not interchangeable.

Atmosphere Monitoring

Use calibrated instruments appropriate to the gas hazard. Check before work and continuously where required.

Ignition Control

Control flames, electrical sparks, static, hot surfaces, mechanical sparks and other listed ignition sources.

Zone Awareness

Zone 0, Zone 1 and Zone 2 indicate different likelihoods of an explosive gas atmosphere being present.
